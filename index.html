Golf league management, made simple

RELM Golf helps golf league organizers run their season, from sign-ups and pairings to scoring, handicaps and standings. Players get a mobile app to enter scores and follow leaderboards live.

What RELM Golf does

For organizers

Seasons, events, registrations, flights and pairings, managed in one place on the web.

Scoring and results

Live leaderboards, net and gross results, and season points standings, updated as scores come in.

For players

An iPhone and Android app to register for events, enter scores on the course and check results.

About us

RELM Golf is built and operated by RELM Golf LLC, a United States company focused on software for amateur golf leagues. The service is in development and opens to leagues soon.
